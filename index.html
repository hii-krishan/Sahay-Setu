Flood Emergency Guide

Floods are one of India's most common disasters. Preparedness saves lives.

BEFORE

Before a Flood

  • 📻 Monitor weather forecasts and official alerts via radio/TV
  • 📦 Prepare an emergency kit: water, food (3 days), medicines, documents
  • 📋 Know your evacuation route and nearest relief camp locations
  • 🔌 Disconnect electrical appliances before leaving home
  • 🏦 Store important documents in waterproof bags
  • 📞 Share your emergency contact info with neighbors
  • 🚗 Move vehicles and valuables to higher ground
  • 💧 Store extra clean drinking water (at least 15L per person)
DURING

During a Flood

  • 🏃 Evacuate immediately if authorities issue evacuation orders
  • ⛔ Do NOT walk, swim, or drive through flood waters
  • ⚡ Stay away from power lines and electrical equipment
  • 📱 Call 112 or report on Sahay-Setu if trapped
  • 🏠 Go to upper floors — do NOT use elevators
  • 🚫 Avoid bridges over fast-moving water
  • 📻 Follow ONLY official instructions
  • 🤝 Help elderly and disabled neighbors if safe to do so
AFTER

After a Flood

  • 🏠 Return home only when authorities confirm it's safe
  • 🔍 Check for structural damage before entering buildings
  • 💧 Do NOT use tap water until declared safe — drink bottled water
  • ⚡ Do not turn on electricity if your home was flooded
  • 🦟 Use mosquito repellent — flood aftermath brings disease risk
  • 📸 Document damage for insurance/relief claims
  • 🧹 Clean and disinfect everything that was in floodwater
  • 📞 Report remaining needs via Sahay-Setu or 112
🌍

Earthquake Emergency Guide

Earthquakes strike without warning. Knowing what to do can save your life.

BEFORE

Before an Earthquake

  • 🔩 Secure heavy furniture and shelves to walls
  • 📦 Prepare an emergency kit with 3-day supplies
  • 🚪 Know the safe spots in each room (under sturdy table, against inner wall)
  • 🔥 Know how to turn off gas, water, and electricity
  • 📋 Practice "Drop, Cover, Hold On" with family
  • 🏠 Check your building's seismic safety rating
DURING

During an Earthquake

  • ⬇️ DROP — get down on hands and knees immediately
  • 🛡️ COVER — get under a sturdy table or cover head/neck with arms
  • ✊ HOLD ON — hold until shaking stops
  • 🚫 Do NOT run outside during shaking
  • 🪟 Stay away from windows, glass, and heavy items
  • 🚗 If in a car, pull over away from buildings and overpasses
  • 🏖️ If on a beach, immediately move inland (tsunami risk)
AFTER

After an Earthquake

  • 🔍 Check for injuries — provide first aid if trained
  • 🔥 Check for fires and gas leaks — shut off gas if smell is detected
  • 📱 Use text messages to conserve phone battery/network
  • 🏚️ Inspect your building for cracks before entering
  • 📻 Monitor emergency broadcasts for aftershock warnings
  • ⛔ Stay away from damaged buildings
  • 📞 Report trapped persons on Sahay-Setu or 112
🔥

Fire Emergency Guide

In a fire, every second counts. Act fast and stay calm.

BEFORE

Fire Prevention & Preparation

  • 🔔 Install smoke alarms on every floor — test monthly
  • 🧯 Keep a fire extinguisher in kitchen and near exits
  • 🚪 Plan and practice two escape routes from every room
  • 📍 Designate a meeting point outside the building
  • ⚡ Don't overload electrical sockets
  • 🕯️ Never leave candles or cooking unattended
DURING

During a Fire

  • 📢 Raise the alarm immediately — shout "FIRE!" and activate alarm
  • 📞 Call 101 (Fire Brigade) immediately
  • 🏃 Evacuate immediately — do NOT stop to collect belongings
  • 🚪 Feel doors before opening — if hot, do NOT open
  • 🌫️ Crawl low under smoke — air is cleaner near the floor
  • 🚫 Never use lifts/elevators during a fire
  • 🛑 If trapped, signal from window with cloth/light
AFTER

After a Fire

  • 🏠 Do NOT re-enter the building until fire service clears it
  • 🩺 Seek medical attention for burns or smoke inhalation
  • 📞 Contact insurance company and document damage
  • 🏠 Report need for temporary shelter via Sahay-Setu
  • 💧 Watch for hot spots that may reignite
🌪️

Cyclone Emergency Guide

Cyclones can be predicted. Use warning time to prepare and evacuate.

BEFORE

Before a Cyclone

  • 📻 Monitor weather updates and cyclone track forecasts
  • 🏠 Reinforce windows and doors with boards or storm shutters
  • 🌳 Trim branches that could fall on your home
  • 📦 Stock 3–7 days of food, water, and medicines
  • 🔦 Keep torches, candles, and battery-powered radios ready
  • 🏥 Know the location of nearest cyclone shelter
  • 🚗 Move inland if you live in a coastal low-lying area
DURING

During a Cyclone

  • 🏠 Stay indoors and away from windows
  • 📻 Monitor official broadcasts — don't go out during the "eye"
  • 🛑 The calm "eye" phase is temporary — storm will resume
  • ⚡ Unplug electrical appliances
  • 🏛️ Move to the strongest room in the house (inner rooms)
  • 🌊 Avoid flooded roads and streams
AFTER

After a Cyclone

  • ⚠️ Wait for official all-clear before going outside
  • ⚡ Avoid downed power lines
  • 💧 Use only safe/bottled water
  • 🏚️ Check structural damage before entering buildings
  • 📸 Document damage for relief applications
  • 📞 Report needs via Sahay-Setu or helpline
🌡️

Heatwave Emergency Guide

Extreme heat is a silent killer. Prevention and hydration are key.

BEFORE

Heatwave Preparation

  • 🌡️ Track heat index, not just temperature
  • 💧 Stock adequate water supplies
  • 🏠 Install fans, cooling equipment, or identify nearby cool spaces
  • 👕 Prepare light, loose-fitting, light-colored clothing
  • 🧴 Stock sunscreen (SPF 30+) and oral rehydration salts (ORS)
DURING

During a Heatwave

  • 💧 Drink 2–3 litres of water/day even if not thirsty
  • ⏰ Avoid going outdoors between 11am–4pm
  • 👕 Wear light, loose, breathable clothing
  • 🌿 Stay in shaded or air-conditioned areas
  • 🍜 Eat small, frequent meals — avoid heavy protein
  • 🚨 Know signs of heat stroke: no sweating, confusion, high temp — Call 108
  • 👴 Check on elderly, children, and outdoor workers regularly
AFTER

After a Heatwave

  • 🩺 Seek medical help for heat-related illness symptoms
  • 💧 Continue hydrating even after the heatwave passes
  • 📞 Report heat-related casualties to authorities
  • 🏥 ORS packets available at relief points — collect if needed
🏚️

Building Collapse Guide

Collapse situations require immediate and calm action.

BEFORE

Building Safety Awareness

  • 🔍 Know the structural health of your building
  • ⚠️ Watch for cracks, unusual noises, or tilting
  • 🚪 Know all exit routes including emergency stairs
  • 📱 Save NDRF and local rescue team numbers
  • 📦 Keep emergency kit accessible
DURING

During / After Collapse

  • 📢 Call 112 immediately if trapped or witnessing collapse
  • 🛑 Do not enter a collapsed or unstable structure
  • 🔦 If trapped: conserve energy, tap pipes/walls to signal rescuers
  • 🚫 Do NOT use lighters or matches — gas leaks may be present
  • 🗣️ Shout only when you hear rescuers — conserve energy otherwise
  • 💨 Cover nose/mouth with cloth to filter dust
AFTER

After a Collapse

  • 🏃 Move away from the collapse zone — secondary collapse risk
  • 🩺 Provide first aid to injured if trained
  • 📞 Report the incident via Sahay-Setu or 112
  • 🚫 Do NOT move seriously injured persons unless in immediate danger
  • 📸 Document and report for NDRF investigation

What is Sahay-Setu?

Sahay-Setu (meaning "Help-Bridge" in Sanskrit/Hindi) is a modern, unified disaster management and relief coordination platform designed for India. It bridges the gap between those who need help during disasters and those who can provide it — Citizens, NGOs, and Government authorities — on a single digital platform.

The name reflects our core mission: to be the bridge ("Setu") that connects vulnerable communities with the right help ("Sahay") at the right time.

⚠️

The Problem We Address

India faces over 800 disasters annually — floods, cyclones, earthquakes, fires, heatwaves — affecting millions of people. Despite the heroic efforts of NGOs, government agencies, and volunteers, the response is often fragmented:

  • Citizens don't know where to report or how to track relief
  • NGOs duplicate efforts in one area while others go unserved
  • Government lacks real-time visibility into ground conditions
  • Resources (food, water, medicine) are misallocated
  • Volunteers are uncoordinated and untracked
  • Donations don't always reach the right cause

The result: help exists, but doesn't reach the right people, at the right place, at the right time.

🎯

Our Vision

A future where no disaster victim is left without help due to coordination failure. Sahay-Setu envisions a connected ecosystem where every citizen can report distress and receive timely assistance, every NGO operates with maximum efficiency, and every government authority has the data they need to make life-saving decisions.

How the Platform Works

1

Citizen Reports

A citizen reports an emergency with location, type, and severity through the Citizen Portal or mobile app.

2

Verification & Routing

The system verifies the report and routes it to relevant NGOs and Government authorities in the area.

3

NGO Coordination

Verified NGOs accept requests, assign volunteers, and deploy resources from their managed inventory.

4

Government Oversight

Government authorities monitor all operations, issue alerts, allocate resources, and verify NGOs in real time.

5

Resolution & Feedback

Once help is delivered, the request is marked resolved. Citizens can rate the response for continuous improvement.
